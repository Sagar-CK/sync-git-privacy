sync-git Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

sync-git is a personal, non-commercial tool built and used only by its owner, Sagar Chethan Kumar, to read his own health and fitness data from the Google Health API (Fitbit) for personal tracking.

Data accessed

Read-only access to the signed-in user's own Google Health data, such as activity, sleep, heart metrics, and nutrition logs. It cannot write, change, or delete any data.

How data is used

Only to produce personal summaries and logs for the owner. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models.

Storage

Data and sign-in tokens are stored privately on the owner's own computer. Nothing is published.

Revoking access

Remove sync-git's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
